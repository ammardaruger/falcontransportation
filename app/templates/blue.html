Welcome to

Blue falcon

Blue Falcon Transportation Company is a dynamic logistics and heavy transport services provider, committed to excellence in moving, hauling, and supporting the region's most critical infrastructure and industrial projects. With a modern fleet, skilled workforce, and a strong safety culture, we serve the construction, oil & gas, and industrial sectors with unwavering reliability.

What we specialize in

We specialize in flatbed logistics, dumper truck operations, heavy equipment rental, tanker transport, and tailored logistics solutions across Saudi Arabia and the GCC. Our success is built on customer trust, operational efficiency, and our dedication to quality service delivery. Whether transporting oversized cargo, bulk materials, or supporting your site with specialized machinery, Blue Falcon stands as your dependable logistics partner
